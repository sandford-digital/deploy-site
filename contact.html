Start with the evidence problem.

If your organisation captures photographs or inspection records that may later be relied upon, challenged or shared with another party, we would like to understand the workflow.

Talk to us about

  • A Property / housing Site Scan pilot
  • Property-service or contractor evidence assurance
  • Inventory and inspection report verification
  • An Evidence SDK integration
  • A specific evidence-integrity or provenance problem

What the first conversation covers

We will ask how evidence is captured today, who relies on it, what happens when it is questioned, what your existing audit trail proves and whether independent verification would add anything useful.

If it does not, that is useful to know too.

There is no requirement to begin with a large implementation.

Pilot approach

Where there is a genuine fit, we prefer to start narrowly: one workflow, a small group of users, a defined volume of inspections and clear learning objectives.
