THE PROBLEM

Most organisations can't prove
what their surveyors actually saw.

When a regulator or insurer asks for contemporaneous evidence of a property inspection, paper forms, spreadsheets, and WhatsApp photos don't hold up.

📋
Paper forms
A paper form carries no verifiable timestamp, no GPS context, and no chain of custody - making it difficult to defend as a contemporaneous record in regulatory proceedings.
📱
WhatsApp photos
Metadata is stripped in transit. No room-level mapping, no hazard classification, no HHSRS linkage. Photographs on a personal device cannot be reliably linked to a survey record.
📊
Spreadsheets
Completed after the fact and subject to error or amendment without trace. Not a contemporaneous record - does not satisfy the evidential requirements of regulatory investigation.
⏰
Delayed reporting
When documentation happens back at the office, time elapses between observation and record - breaking the chain of custody at the point it matters most.

PLATFORM FEATURES

Built for housing associations.
Built for compliance.

GPS-stamped chain of custody
Every capture carries GPS coordinates, a verified timestamp, and a session ID - written to a tamper-evident cloud record aligned with BS 10008 principles.
AI hazard assessment
Each frame is assessed against HHSRS categories at the point of capture. The correct Awaab's Law SLA threshold is assigned automatically - no surveyor training required.
Awaab's Law compliance engine
Emergency hazards trigger an immediate on-screen alert. Statutory deadlines are calculated and appear in the PDF report from the moment of identification.
Offline-first operation
Surveys complete fully offline and sync when connectivity returns. Works in basement flats and properties with no signal. No specialist hardware required.
HHSRS functional checklist
21 hazard categories per room - boiler safety, carbon monoxide alarm, structural checks - with surveyor notes attached to each finding and included in the report.
Instant PDF report
A structured survey report generated and shared from the device before the surveyor leaves the property. No post-survey data entry. No transcription errors.

Awaab's Law is not optional.

From October 2025, registered social housing providers must investigate hazards within legally defined windows - and demonstrate compliance with a documented, timestamped evidence trail. Sandford Site Scan creates that trail automatically, at the moment of capture.

24 Hours
Emergency hazards - make-safe works must begin within 24 hours of identification. Sandford Site Scan alerts the surveyor immediately and sets the deadline in the evidence record.
10 Days
Significant hazards - investigation must begin within 10 working days. Deadline calculated automatically at point of capture and included in the PDF report.
Routine
Lower-risk defects documented, classified against HHSRS categories, and scheduled for planned maintenance - with a full audit trail from capture to resolution.
