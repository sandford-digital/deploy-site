Capture the inspection. Protect the evidence.

Sandford Site Scan is a field inspection application from Sandford Digital for organisations that need a structured record of what was seen, where it was seen and when it was captured.

The difference comes afterwards: the evidence chain can be independently verified.

From field capture to independently verifiable evidence

Site Scan combines the practical workflow of a mobile inspection application with an evidence-integrity layer designed for situations where the inspection may later be reviewed, challenged or relied upon.

  • Structured inspection workflow
  • Property and location context
  • Room-by-room photographic capture
  • Automatic timestamp and GPS capture
  • Offline-first field operation
  • Tamper-evident evidence chain
  • Cryptographic public anchoring
  • Shareable PDF reporting
  • Public, unauthenticated chain verification

What Site Scan proves and what it does not

Sandford Digital is deliberately precise about the claim.

The cryptographic chain is designed to demonstrate the integrity of the evidence and metadata bound into it, including captured evidence, time, location and chain order.

Inspector identity and organisational context are operational attribution controls. Sandford Digital does not claim that a cryptographic hash alone proves that a particular individual personally created an item of evidence.

Site Scan supports better evidence integrity. It does not replace professional judgement, statutory duties or legal advice.
