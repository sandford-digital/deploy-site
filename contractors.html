The gap between doing the work and proving the work

Property-service contracts generate photographs, job notes, completion records and client reports. Those records are valuable, but they are usually held inside systems controlled by one of the parties involved.

Sandford Digital adds a separate evidence-integrity layer, allowing captured evidence to be checked against public cryptographic proof later.

Where it can fit

  • Initial property or defect condition
  • Pre-work evidence
  • Work-in-progress evidence
  • Completion and handback records
  • Subcontractor assurance
  • Client queries and contract reviews
  • Repeat repairs and disputed outcomes
