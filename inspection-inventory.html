Your report may be independent. Now the evidence can be independently verifiable too.

Sandford Digital adds a tamper-evident evidence chain and public verification to photographic inspection and inventory workflows.

Become a design partner

Evidence is the product

Inventory and inspection businesses already compete on thoroughness, consistency, independence and the quality of their photographic records.

Sandford Digital tests a further proposition: does giving the recipient a way to verify that the digital evidence has not changed create additional trust or commercial value?

What the client sees

The inspection is completed and the report is issued as normal. A QR code or Session ID connects the recipient to Sandford Digital's public Chain-Verification Tool.

The verifier re-derives the evidence chain and checks it against the public cryptographic anchor.

The result is a report that does not simply ask the recipient to trust the software provider's database.

Potential uses

  • Check-in and check-out reports
  • Property inventories
  • Condition reports
  • Independent inspection services
  • Handovers
  • Evidence packages prepared for later review or dispute
