02 // THE SHORT VERSION

Sandford Site Scan is designed to inspect assets such as buildings, vehicles and rail equipment, not people. It is not designed to identify, track or profile individuals. We take steps to reduce the risk that people appear in survey photos, but we cannot remove that risk: people, voices and personal items can still appear in photos, video and sound. This policy explains what we do about that.

  • Where the app finds a face in a photo, it covers the face with a solid block on the phone before the photo is saved or sent. The surveyor can also cover a letter, screen or name label by hand. The original photo is not kept. This reduces the risk that people appear in stored photos; it does not remove it
  • Photos taken in a survey are stored, with faces covered, as part of the survey record. Unredacted originals of photos are not kept. Video is optional. Video clips are stored as recorded and are not covered, and in the Housing app they are recorded without sound unless the surveyor turns Sound on. Where sound is recorded it may capture voices, so the app asks the surveyor to confirm whether sound is required before filming
  • The first frame of each video is covered automatically, with the same face check as photos, before it is sent for AI checking. If the check cannot run, that frame is not sent
  • Location is optional. If the surveyor allows it, each photo and video records where it was taken, including the phone's raw height reading
  • For customer survey data, the customer decides how the data is used and we act on its instructions. We do not use customer survey data for our own purposes other than providing, securing and supporting the service
  • We do not sell personal data to third parties under any circumstances
  • You have rights over your personal data, including to access, correct and ask for erasure of it (Section 08). Where a customer is the controller, we pass your request to the customer

04 // HOW WE USE AI VISION

Sandford Site Scan uses Claude, an AI model developed by Anthropic, to give an advisory assessment of asset condition from photos and video. A shrunk copy of each photo, after covering, and one still frame from each video clip, also covered, are submitted to Anthropic's API over an encrypted connection (TLS), through our own server. Only the picture and the room name and area label are passed to Anthropic. We do not send the location, address, surveyor name or customer. Anthropic's data processing terms apply to this transfer.

The first frame of a video is put through the same face check as a photo before it is sent. If the check cannot run, the frame is not sent. The video itself is not sent to Anthropic.

We instruct the AI model to:

  • Assess the physical condition of the asset surface visible in the frame
  • Identify damage, wear, or anomalies against a known baseline condition
  • Return a structured JSON assessment: severity, findings, confidence, recommendation
  • Describe only observable physical conditions of the property, and make no comment on occupants, housekeeping, belongings or pets

We do not ask the AI to identify or describe people. People can still appear in a photo, because the face covering can miss a face and does not cover bodies, voices or other things that identify someone. Before the first photo or video, the app shows this notice: "Photos, and a still picture from each video, are sent to Anthropic's AI service in the United States to check for defects. Keep faces, family photos, letters, screens and name labels out of frame." Before filming, the app also asks the surveyor to confirm whether sound is required.

We also use Anthropic's API for a second purpose. It sorts replies from our business contacts (Section 03, part E). For that, the subject and body of the reply are sent to Anthropic. This is our own business email, not survey data.

Anthropic keeps what we send, for both purposes, under its own terms. At the time of writing, its published position is that it deletes inputs and outputs within 30 days of receipt, and may keep them for up to two years where its systems flag content as breaching its usage policy. We do not have a zero retention agreement with Anthropic. We do not keep the copies sent to Anthropic. We keep the AI assessment output as part of the survey record.

The AI result is advisory. It is shown to the surveyor and in the report as AI-assisted triage, not as an inspection finding, and it is not part of the tamper-evident record. We do not use it to make decisions about individuals. Decisions about repairs or action are made by the customer's staff. Our use of AI is assistive, not determinative.

05 // OFFLINE PROCESSING

Sandford Site Scan is designed for use where there is little or no signal, including depots, basements and underground facilities. When network connectivity is unavailable:

  • Photos and video are taken, faces are covered on the phone, and the photos, video and survey details are stored on the phone in a queue
  • The AI check cannot run offline. Photos are checked when the phone is back online, and until then the report shows them as not checked
  • Nothing is sent until the phone has a network connection. The queue then sends to our servers automatically
  • Survey data stays on the phone, in the app, after it has been sent, until the app is removed. There is no clear-data button in the app, and the app adds no encryption of its own on the phone (see Section 09)

07 // HOW LONG WE KEEP DATA

TRACK A: EVIDENCE CONTENT

Evidence content is the substance of a survey: photos, video, sound, notes and answers, location, spatial and session metadata, and AI assessment output.

  • Survey records (photos, video and sound, notes and answers, location with height, address, session details and AI assessment output) are kept for six years from the date of the survey, then deleted. We review our records at least once a year to find any that are due for deletion
  • Earlier if the customer instructs, or if a valid erasure request requires it.
  • Audit log, device identifiers and technical logs are generally kept for 90 days. Some are deleted automatically. Where that is not yet automatic, we delete them by hand at least once every 12 months
  • The device identifier recorded in a survey record stays with that record
  • Unredacted original photos are not kept. The covered photo is the record
  • Surveyor, organisation and portal account details and name: account details are kept until the account is deleted. We delete any account that has not been used for six years, and we review accounts at least once a year to find them
  • Contact form record: 12 months (see part D). Business contacts: [SOLICITOR TO COMPLETE]
  • Data on the phone: stays in the app until the app is removed
  • Copies sent to Anthropic: held under Anthropic's own terms (Section 04)

Survey records are kept for six years from the date of the survey. This matches the limitation period for most claims in England and Wales (Limitation Act 1980) and our customers' own record-keeping duties. As processor we follow the customer's written instruction, so a customer may instruct earlier deletion or return, within 30 days of termination. Records are deleted after the period ends, or sooner on instruction (see Section 08). Where a customer asks us to delete or return records, we will do so within one month of the request.

Backups and recovery features of our hosting provider may keep deleted data for up to 7 days after deletion.

TRACK B: TAMPER-EVIDENT FINGERPRINT

We create a tamper-evident fingerprint of every sealed survey record, a short code. The fingerprints of the records sealed since the last run are combined into one short code, which we publish permanently on a public blockchain (Arbitrum One) at frequent intervals, currently nightly and expected to become more frequent, so a very recent record may not yet be published. A sealed record covers the photos, the location (including the phone's raw height reading) and time of capture, the surveyor's name, and the surveyor's answers and notes. The content stays in our own secure database; only the fingerprint is published, and no content or personal data is on the blockchain. What the fingerprint does is let someone, such as a regulator, an insurer or a court, check that the sealed record in our database has not been quietly changed since it was sealed, without having to take our word for it. If a value in the record is erased (for example, if someone asks us to remove their data), the fingerprint remains on the blockchain, but the record can no longer be verified against it. The fingerprint only works as proof for as long as the record it checks still exists unchanged.

08 // YOUR RIGHTS

Under UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, you have the following rights:

  • Access — request a copy of personal data we hold about you
  • Rectification — request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Erasure — request deletion of your personal data where there is no legitimate reason for continued processing
  • Restriction — request that we restrict processing of your personal data in certain circumstances
  • Portability — request transfer of your personal data to another controller in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Automated decisions — you have the right not to be subject to solely automated decisions that have legal or significant effects on you

Data we control. For our own data (Section 01), contact us at privacy@sandford.digital. We will reply within one month.

Survey data. Where a customer is the controller, your request should go to the customer. If you send it to us, we will pass it to the customer within 5 working days and tell you we have done so. Our records are organised by property, not by person, and we do not search them by person, so it helps to tell us the property and the approximate date of the survey.

Deleting an account. A surveyor can ask for their account and data to be deleted from the home screen of the app ("Request deletion of my account and data"), or by emailing privacy@sandford.digital. We will reply within one month, saying what we will delete, what we have to keep and why, and when. We may have to keep survey records that you helped create, including your name as recorded in them, because they are sealed evidence your organisation relies on (kept for six years from the date of the survey), and the fingerprints of those records that are published on a public blockchain, which hold no personal details and cannot be changed (see Section 07).

Location. You can decline location in the app, and turn it off again in the phone's settings.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal data, please tell us at privacy@sandford.digital. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you what we find. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

09 // SECURITY

  • Data is encrypted in transit using TLS
  • Data held in Google Cloud is encrypted at rest by Google. The app adds no encryption of its own on the phone, so data stored on the phone relies on the phone's own protections
  • Our servers check the surveyor's sign-in on every request. Access is controlled by organisation and role. We are moving every account into an organisation so that each customer's survey data is kept separate from other customers', and accounts created before that move are being transferred
  • We use Google's App Check to check that requests come from the genuine app. It is currently in monitoring mode, which records requests that fail the check but does not yet refuse them. We also limit repeated requests
  • Secret keys for our servers, including the key for the AI service, are held in Google's secret storage on the server, not in the app.
  • Access to client data is restricted to authorised personnel only
  • We keep an audit log of administration actions
  • We conduct regular security reviews of our infrastructure and codebase
