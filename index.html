Maz Hayes

Maz Hayes is the Founder of Sandford Digital Limited, a B2B digital transformation consultancy working with organisations navigating complex structural and technological change. She brings 20+ years of senior digital leadership across UK regulated industries - including Head of Digital roles at RBS and Bank of Ireland UK, Associate Partner at IBM iX, Managing Partner at WPP, and Senior Strategic Sales Director at Adobe.

Alongside the consultancy, Maz is building Sandford Site Scan - a patent-pending spatial triage SDK that turns any mobile device into a structured, AI-powered asset inspection tool. The product addresses a problem hiding in plain sight across rail, fleet, and facilities: physical asset inspection runs on clipboards and WhatsApp, with no spatial context, no baseline, and no audit trail. Sandford Site Scan is the infrastructure layer that nobody built.
