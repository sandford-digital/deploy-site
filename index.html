A report is a claim. The evidence behind it is the proof.

Property inspections, repairs, contractor visits and condition reports increasingly depend on photographs and digital records. But when that evidence matters months later, an important question remains: can the recipient demonstrate that the evidence they are looking at is the same evidence that was originally captured?

Sandford Digital is built around that question.

How Sandford Digital works

  1. 1. Capture

    Inspectors use a standard mobile device to capture structured evidence in the field. The Property workflow supports addresses, inspection scope, rooms, issues and photographic evidence, and is designed to continue working when connectivity is poor.

  2. 2. Bind

    Captured evidence is cryptographically bound to its timestamp, GPS data and the previous event in the inspection chain. This creates an ordered, tamper-evident record of capture.

  3. 3. Anchor

    Cryptographic proof is periodically anchored to a public blockchain. The underlying inspection photographs and personal information are not published to the blockchain.

  4. 4. Report

    The inspection produces a shareable PDF report with the inspection summary and its Sandford Digital Session ID.

  5. 5. Verify

    A recipient can scan the report QR code and use Sandford Digital's public Chain-Verification Tool. The verifier reconstructs the evidence chain and checks it against the public anchor.

    They do not have to take Sandford Digital's word for it.

Private evidence. Public proof.

The evidence itself remains off-chain. Only cryptographic proof is publicly anchored. This keeps the inspection data private while allowing its integrity to be checked independently.
