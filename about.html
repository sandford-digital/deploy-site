Building digital evidence that can be checked, not just trusted.

Sandford Digital is a UK technology company focused on the integrity of digital evidence created during physical inspections and field activity.

Why we built it

Organisations increasingly make important decisions using photographs, videos and digital inspection records. Capturing the evidence is only one part of the problem.

The harder question often comes later: when somebody else relies on that evidence, how do they know it is still the evidence that was originally captured?

Sandford Digital was built to make that question easier to answer.

Our approach

We combine practical field capture with cryptographic evidence chaining and public proof anchoring.

The goal is not to put private evidence on a blockchain. The goal is to keep private evidence private while making its integrity independently checkable.

Private evidence. Public proof.

Built for real workflows

Sandford Digital's technology is designed to work across sectors where photographs and field evidence matter. Site Scan currently supports workflow-specific experiences including Property, Vehicle, Film & TV Locations and Rail, with current commercial focus on Property.

The same evidence-integrity capability can also be integrated into existing products through the Sandford Digital Evidence SDK.

Founder

Sandford Digital was founded by Maz Hayes, a digital leader with more than 20 years' experience spanning technology, product, digital strategy and delivery, including leadership roles in banking and consulting.

That background shapes the company: build technology that solves a specific operational problem, be precise about what it proves, and design for organisations that need trust, governance and practical delivery rather than technology theatre.
